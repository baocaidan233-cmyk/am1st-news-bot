2. "d" — WHAT WAS DONE

  stopped    our side took something back, enforced something, won it: a ruling
             our way, an arrest, a deportation, a law passed, a program killed
  price      someone on their side is paying for it with no action from us: they
             resigned, got fired, lost the money, got humiliated, went under
  happening  it is still being done to us and nothing has stopped it
  none       nothing was done either way — an event reported, a step in a
             process, a hearing, a filing, a poll, a statement, a plan for later

3. "who" — WHOSE NAME IS IN IT

  opponent   a specific person on the other side: doing it, or being held to
             account for it. A named person, or an unmistakable single role
             ("the judge who released him"). Not a party, agency or country.
  ally       a specific person on our side: fighting it, winning it, or being
             targeted for it
  ordinary   a specific ordinary person it happened to — a victim, a parent, a
             worker, a neighbour
  none       no specific person at all, only institutions

  If more than one fits, answer with the one the story is really about.

4. "drop" — THE ONLY HARD REJECTIONS

  anti_admin  the story's own frame attacks President Trump, his administration,

  trivia      sports, weather, celebrity gossip, a product launch, a market move,

  none        anything else, including stories you think are unimportant, and
